## Bio

A decade in San Miguel de Allende, most of it spent at taco carts, in aging cantinas, and down side streets with the phone in my pocket. I write guides to the places I keep going back to: street food, cold lagers, old bones, owner-operated spots, and strange secrets.

Anthropology always.
