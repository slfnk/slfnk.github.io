# FAQ

Guiides are hand-written guides to places, each one made by a person who knows it well. A few answers to common questions:

## What is Guiides?

One person's picks for a place, written up in their own words, with a map that follows along. No star ratings, no ads, and no algorithm deciding what rises to the top.

## Who writes the guides?

The person named in each guide's byline. Every pick and every word comes from them.

## Why not just use Google Maps or TripAdvisor?

They're great for opening hours and directions. But paid placement and fake reviews have made their rankings hard to trust. A guide from someone with a point of view tells you what's actually worth your time.

## How do I save spots?

Tap the bookmark on any spot, then use the **Saved** filter to see just your picks. Saved spots live in your browser on that device, so clearing your browser data or using a private window will reset them.

## Why do some guides ask for a password?

Some authors share their guides privately. If you were given a link, the author can give you the password too.

## Can I make my own guide?

Not yet. Guiides is still in its early days. Check back soon.
