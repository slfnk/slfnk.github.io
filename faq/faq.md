# FAQ

## What is Guiides?

Guiides is a free tool to build and share mapped local knowledge and opinions. The emphasis is on authored personal writing to share opinions, and less on photos and videos. The tool aims to be a speedy and lightweight web-app that is instantly usable on a friend's phone with no logins or app downloads.

## Why .Org?

Guiides is spiritually aligned with an older age on the internet, where data-mining and revenue flow isn't the primary mission. 

## Why not just share Google Maps or docs with friends and followers?

There are some frustrating limitations to Google Maps, My Maps and Google Docs that this tool seeks to improve upon — and to do so without harvesting you and your friend's data for ad-targeting. The tool aims to emulate the feeling of an authored guidebook that you might purchase in a bookstore. 

## When can I try Guiides myself?

The tool is currently in testing trials and should be open for general users soon. Please get in touch if you would like early access to the platform.
