# FAQ

## What is Guiides?

Guiides is a free tool to build and share local knowledge. The emphasis is on authored personal writing to share opinions, and less on photos and videos. The tool aims to be a speedy and lightweight web-app that is instantly usable on a friend's phone with no logins or app downloads.

## Why .Org?

Guiides is spiritually aligned with an older age on the internet, where data-mining and revenue flow isn't the primary mission.

## Why not just share Google Maps or docs with friends and followers?

There are some frustrating limitations to Google Maps, My Maps and Google Docs that this tool seeks to improve upon — and to do so without harvesting you and your friend's data. The tool aims to feel more like an authored guidebook.

## When can I try Guiides myself?

The tool is currently in the testing phase and should be open to limited followers soon.
